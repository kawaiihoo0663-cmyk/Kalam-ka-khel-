THE IDEA

Knowing the answer
is only half the game.

Inside an exam hall, you are making dozens of small decisions. Which question first? How long is too long? What deserves a diagram? When should you move on?

Kalam Ka Khel lets you rehearse those decisions before the real paper, one low-pressure scenario at a time.
